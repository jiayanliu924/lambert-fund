    The firm

    A small fund with a narrow mandate.

    The fund exists to do one thing: own meaningful stakes in the few AI companies that will still matter in ten years, and hold them.

    Lambert Fund was founded in 2026 in Palo Alto by Ramble, an investor who has backed Silicon Valley's frontier AI companies across successive rounds — and who continues to invest his own capital in every position the fund takes.

    We do not run a broad portfolio. We do not chase themes. We do not invest outside Silicon Valley.

    Approach

    Few companies. Large positions. Long holds.

    Concentration is not a risk we tolerate. It is the strategy.

    I

    Concentration over coverage

    A handful of companies rather than a hundred. We would rather own more of the right company than a little of everything, and we size positions accordingly.

    II

    The leader, not the alternative

    In every category we back the company that is already ahead. We do not fund the second-best at a discount, and we do not invest outside Silicon Valley, whatever the price.

    III

    Bought to hold

    We follow our companies round after round and hold through the IPO and beyond. Our time horizon is the company's, not the fund cycle's.

    IV

    Access, not just capital

    The best companies rarely raise in public. We reach them through direct allocations, long relationships with the venture firms that lead their rounds, and secondary purchases from early shareholders.

    Where we look

    Three layers of the AI economy.

    The models, the substrate beneath them, and the machines they will run.

    The modelsFrontier labs

    The laboratories building the most capable models in the world and becoming the default interface between people, organizations and intelligence. The core of the portfolio.

    The substrateAI infrastructure

    The compute, cloud and systems companies that every lab depends on and every application pays for — the layer that gets paid whoever wins the model race.

    The machinesPhysical AI & deep tech

    Humanoid robotics, launch, and the frontier of hard science. Where a category leader is still emerging, we take an early position and follow the winner.
